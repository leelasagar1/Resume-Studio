Analytics Engineer
Juniper Freight

Juniper Freight is rebuilding its data stack. As our second Analytics Engineer you will turn raw logistics data into models analysts trust.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain dbt models in Snowflake for shipments, carriers and billing.
- Design dimensional data models and document them.
- Write data quality tests and monitor data freshness.
- Orchestrate pipelines with Airflow.
- Partner with analysts on Tableau and Power BI dashboards.
- Tune SQL performance and warehouse cost.

What you bring
- 4+ years in analytics engineering, data engineering or analytics.
- Expert SQL and solid Python.
- Experience with dbt and a cloud data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift).
- Experience with data modeling, ETL/ELT and version control with Git.
- Clear communication with business stakeholders.

Nice to have: Spark, logistics experience, experience with Fivetran or Airbyte.
