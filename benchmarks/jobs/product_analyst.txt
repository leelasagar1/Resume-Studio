Product Analyst, Growth
Tidewater Apps

Tidewater makes a budgeting app used by 2 million people. The growth team needs an analyst who can say what happened, why, and what to try next.

Responsibilities
- Define and track product metrics for activation, retention and monetization.
- Design experiments with product managers and analyse A/B tests with sound statistics.
- Build funnel analysis and cohort analysis in SQL.
- Create self-serve dashboards in tools such as Amplitude, Mixpanel or Looker.
- Use Python or R for deeper statistical analysis, including regression and causal inference.
- Present insights and recommendations to product leadership.

Qualifications
- 3+ years in product analytics or data analysis.
- Strong SQL.
- Experience with experimentation and statistical significance.
- Experience with a product analytics tool.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management.

Preferred: experience in consumer mobile apps, pricing experiments, BigQuery.
