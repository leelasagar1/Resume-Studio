Data Analyst
Fernhill Subscriptions

We sell subscription pet food to 250,000 households. The analytics team is four people and sits next to growth and finance.

In this role you will:
- Own retention and churn reporting and present findings to the growth team each week.
- Write SQL against our BigQuery warehouse and build dashboards in Looker.
- Design, run and read A/B tests with product managers.
- Build cohort analysis and customer lifetime value models with finance.
- Automate recurring reports in Python.
- Maintain data documentation and data quality checks with analytics engineering (dbt).

You have:
- 3+ years of experience as a data analyst.
- Advanced SQL and working Python.
- Experience with a BI tool (Looker or Tableau).
- A track record of turning analysis into decisions with stakeholders.
- A bachelor's degree in a quantitative field.

Bonus: dbt, experimentation platforms, subscription or e-commerce experience.
