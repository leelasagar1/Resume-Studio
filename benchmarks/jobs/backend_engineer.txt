Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
Corvid Pay

Corvid Pay moves money for 40,000 small businesses. You will join the six-person ledger team.

What you will do
- Design and build Go services that record and reconcile payments.
- Own API design for internal and partner-facing REST and gRPC APIs.
- Improve reliability: idempotency, retries, observability and incident response.
- Work with PostgreSQL at scale: schema design, query tuning and migrations.
- Build event-driven systems on Kafka.
- Take part in the on-call rotation and write postmortems.
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews.

What we look for
- 6+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Strong Go or another statically typed language.
- Experience with distributed systems and microservices on AWS.
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes and Terraform.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines.

Nice to have
- Payments or fintech experience, PCI DSS knowledge.
- Experience with Redis and gRPC.
